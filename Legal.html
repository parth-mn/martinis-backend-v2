Section 01

Privacy Policy

How your information is handled

Introduction

Lipsticks & Martinis / L&M respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you visit our website, subscribe to our newsletter, contact us, use the L&M Journal, read editorial content, shop through curated links, or interact with the L&M world in any other way.

What information we collect

  • Your name and email address when you subscribe or contact us.
  • Messages, brand details, company name, and inquiry information submitted through forms.
  • Basic website analytics such as device type, browser type, pages visited, and general usage data.
  • L&M Journal activity or preferences when you use journal features, mood tracking, or check-ins.
  • Any other information you choose to share with us voluntarily.

How we use your information

1

To send what you asked for

Newsletters, Martini Mondays, issue drops, replies, and requested updates.

2

To respond properly

Contact form messages, brand outreach, PR inquiries, and Studio leads.

3

To improve the platform

Understanding what readers use, what pages perform well, and how the experience can be made better.

4

To support L&M Journal

Maintaining features such as check-ins, trackers, saved progress, or similar web app functions.

Cookies, analytics, and email preferences

We may use cookies and analytics tools to understand traffic, improve content performance, and refine the website experience. You can typically manage cookies through your browser settings. If you subscribe to emails, you may unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link in the email or by contacting us directly.

How information may be shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers that help us run L&M, such as website hosting tools, email platforms, analytics tools, and web app providers. Some pages may also contain affiliate links, editorial recommendations, PR placements, or brand collaborations. If you purchase through certain links, L&M may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Data protection and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your information and keep it only for as long as necessary to operate the website, respond to inquiries, deliver services, or meet legal and operational requirements. No method of storing or transmitting data online is completely secure, but we aim to treat your information with care.

Your rights

  • You may request access to the information we hold about you.
  • You may ask us to correct, update, or delete your information where appropriate.
  • You may withdraw consent or opt out of communications at any time.
  • You may contact us with privacy questions or concerns.

Children's privacy and updates to this policy

L&M is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, and the most current version will always appear on this page.
