What kind of founder or brand is L&M Studio best for?

The studio is best for early-stage founders, D2C brands, and women-led businesses that already have an idea, product, or direction — and now need their brand to look more coherent, premium, and ready.

What if I need both strategy and design, not just one deliverable?

That is exactly where the studio works best. The stronger projects usually need both: a clearer story, sharper positioning, and visuals that express it properly across decks, social assets, and launch materials.

What do you need from me before we begin?

You do not need a perfect brief. You just need enough clarity on what you are building, who it is for, and what you need the design to help you achieve. The first call helps shape the rest.

Can you work with a partial brand, existing logo, or half-built deck?

Yes. Some founders come in at zero, others already have pieces in place. L&M Studio can build from scratch or refine what already exists into something stronger, more cohesive, and more usable.

What happens after the project is delivered?

You receive final files that are meant to be used, not admired once. If needed, the studio can also continue with follow-up support for launch assets, social systems, deck updates, or future campaign pieces.
