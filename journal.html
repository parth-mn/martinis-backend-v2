A Few Things You May Be Wondering

Journal FAQs

What is L&M Journal? +

L&M Journal is a browser-based companion to the L&M world, designed to help you bring the ideas you read in the magazine into your everyday life. It brings together gentle routines, mood check-ins, hydration tracking, magazine-inspired rituals and collectible main-character charms in one thoughtful digital space.

Do I need to download anything? +

No. The first version of L&M Journal will work directly in your browser, so there is no app to download. You will be able to access it on both mobile and desktop through your personal Journal account.

When does it launch? +

L&M Journal launches on Saturday, 19 September 2026. A small group of selected beta testers will receive access before the public launch.

What will be available at launch? +

The first release will include the Morning Checklist, Mood Tracker, Water Tracker and Magazine Rituals, along with six collectible main-character charms. We are keeping the first version intentionally focused so that every feature feels useful, reliable and genuinely delightful to return to.

How can I join the beta? +

You can apply for beta access through the short application form on this page. A small number of applicants will be selected to test the Journal before launch, explore its first features and share honest feedback. Applying does not guarantee selection, but all applicants can receive important Journal and launch updates.

How is this different from Notion or a regular habit tracker? +

You do not have to design, organise or build the system yourself. L&M Journal is a guided experience connected to the L&M editorial universe, with carefully created rituals, emotional check-ins and tools that work together. It is designed to feel personal and beautiful without turning your life into another productivity project.

Will my entries be private? +

Yes. Your personal check-ins and Journal activity will be connected to your authenticated account and will not be publicly visible to other users. Clear information about how your data is stored, accessed and protected will be provided in the L&M Journal Privacy Policy before launch.

Is L&M Journal free to use? +

The pricing and access structure for the public version will be announced closer to launch. Selected beta testers will receive access to the testing version without charge during the beta period.

What happens after I apply for beta access? +

Once you submit the application, your response will be reviewed by the L&M team. Selected testers will be contacted by email with their private access link, testing dates and simple instructions. Applicants who are not selected may still receive product updates and the public launch link.

Will more features be added later? +

Yes. The first release is only the beginning. New tools, rituals and experiences may be introduced gradually based on how people use the Journal and the feedback shared during beta testing. We would rather grow thoughtfully than fill the Journal with features that do not meaningfully improve your experience.
