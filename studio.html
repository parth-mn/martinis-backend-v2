Before you enquire

The practical questions.

Exact timeline confirmed after reviewing service, quantity and material readiness.

Can I combine two services?
Yes. Related scopes combine into one proposal, but each stays separately costed so deliverables don't go vague. A campaign concept can pair with collection copy or an editorial lookbook.
Do I need a logo already?
For most services, yes. L&M Studio works within an existing visual identity for most services; for a new logo or full identity system, see Full Brand Identity below.
Will the Studio produce the campaign too?
The campaign concept service ends at creative direction, messaging, and sample compositions. Photography, shoot management and publishing aren't in the base scope.
Can you work with international clients?
Yes. Calls, feedback and handover happen online, and international quotations follow the agreed scope and payment method.
What does "three revision rounds" mean?
One complete, internally approved feedback list per round. Scattered messages or new directions after approval count as extra revisions or a scope change.
What does "starting scope" mean?
The exact deliverables covered by the displayed price. Larger quantities or formats get a customised scope and quotation, confirmed in writing before work begins.
How long will my project take?
Each service lists a typical window. Timing starts once the proposal's accepted, advance received, kickoff done, and materials supplied; feedback wait time isn't counted.
How can I contact the Studio?
One organised email thread for approvals and files, plus a WhatsApp chat for quick clarifications. Replies within one business day; meetings scheduled separately when useful.
