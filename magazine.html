A Look Inside Issue 04

What we're figuring out with you

There is no syllabus for becoming an adult. Issue 04 brings together ten women and 90 pages of essays, anonymous questions, cultural criticism, visual exercises and honest reminders for the years nobody quite knows what they’re doing.

The Plan, Rewritten

Five-year plans meet changing minds, uncertain careers, internet timelines and the quiet progress no conventional CV knows how to measure.

The Person, Still Becoming

Confidence, identity, femininity and girlhood, explored through all the small ways we grow into women our younger selves might not recognise.

The Maintenance Department

Appointments, domestic learning, beauty rituals and realistic resets for keeping a life functioning without turning care into another performance.

The People We Keep

Adult friendships, complicated goodbyes, deliberate effort and the boundaries that sometimes look like endings but feel like choosing yourself.

The Price of Choosing Yourself

Money, privilege, safety nets and the complicated courage of betting on yourself before your choices begin paying you back.

The Almost-Adult Toolkit

Anonymous Office Hours, an invisible-progress CV, friendship calendars, self-care bingo, money calculators, song receipts and proof that you’re doing better than you think.
