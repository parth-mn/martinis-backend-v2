Frequently asked

Questions brands usually ask first.

A soft little clarity section — so collaborations feel considered, not confusing.

How can a brand collaborate with L&M?
L&M works with brands through considered editorial collaborations, issue-led storytelling, Martini Mondays, curated product discovery, and select placements within the L&M Universe. Every collaboration is approached through the lens of taste, relevance, and fit — so the brand feels like it belongs inside the world, not beside it.
What kinds of brands are a fit for L&M?
L&M is drawn to brands that feel intentional, feminine, useful, beautiful, and emotionally resonant. Fashion, beauty, books, accessories, lifestyle, home, rituals, cafés, travel, wellness, and thoughtful digital products are natural fits — especially when there is a strong story, aesthetic, or everyday-use value behind them.
Do you accept PR packages?
Yes. L&M is open to discovering products, experiences, and brands that align with the world we are building. If you would like to introduce something to L&M, send a short note about the brand, the product, and why you feel it belongs in the L&M universe.
Do you collaborate with creators, publications, or podcasts?
Yes. L&M is open to thoughtful conversations and collaborations with creators, writers, editors, publications, podcasts, and platforms that speak to women, becoming, lifestyle, beauty, fashion, independence, creativity, or culture.
What should I include in my inquiry?
Please include who you are, what you are building, the brand or platform link, the kind of collaboration you have in mind, your timeline, and any relevant context. The clearest notes make it easier to understand the fit and respond with the right next step.
